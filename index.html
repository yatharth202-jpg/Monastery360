Differentiator layer

Preserve & protect — the trust & safety layer

This is the part almost no competing team builds: a transparent funding ledger, a citizen preservation watch, and real digital fingerprinting for artifact provenance — reframing decay reporting and fingerprinting explicitly as heritage security.

Adopt-a-Artifact — transparent preservation ledger

Each pledge is chained to the previous one's hash (SHA-256), so any tampering with past entries is instantly detectable — a lightweight, honest stand-in for a real blockchain ledger.

Citizen Preservation Watch

Locals and tourists become the eyes protecting the site — report cracks, leaks, or fading paint the moment they're spotted, instead of waiting for a scheduled inspection.

Digital fingerprinting — artifact anti-theft & provenance

Upload a photo of an artifact to generate a genuine SHA-256 perceptual-style fingerprint, computed live in your browser (Web Crypto API — no server, no upload). Registering a fingerprint creates a provenance record; re-checking a photo against the registry demonstrates how tampering or substitution would be caught.
